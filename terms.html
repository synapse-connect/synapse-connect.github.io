Dating safely

SYNAPSE DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND SCREENINGS OF ITS MEMBERS.

We check professional credentials and compare a selfie with a photo ID, but these checks do not reveal criminal history. Use your own judgment with everyone you meet.

  • Profiles can be faked. Someone willing to steal an identity can also fake a dating profile, even a verified one. Be cautious with anyone you have not met.
  • Keep personal details private. Leave your last name, home address, phone number, workplace and email out of your profile and early messages. Keep the conversation in Synapse until you trust the person.
  • Never send money. Do not send money, gift cards or financial details to someone you met online, however convincing the reason. Report anyone who asks.
  • Meet in public. For a first meeting, choose a busy public place, arrange your own transportation, and never agree to be picked up at home.
  • Tell someone your plans. Let a friend or family member know who you are meeting, where you will be and when you expect to be back.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, leave. You can unmatch, block or report anyone at any time, and in an emergency call 911.

Report or block anyone in the app, or email support@synapsematches.com. In an emergency, call 911.
