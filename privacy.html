Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Who we are

Synapse Connect LLC ("Synapse," "we," "us") runs the Synapse dating app for healthcare workers. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Contact: support@synapsematches.com.

2. What we collect

You give us:

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, date of birth, gender, what you're looking for, and who you want to see.
  • Professional details: occupation and specialty; your NPI number, or your license number, state, expiry date and a photo of the license; or for students, a .edu email or a student ID photo.
  • Identity check: a photo of your government ID and a selfie (see section 4).
  • Profile: photos, prompt answers, and optional details such as hometown, political views, kids, pets, zodiac sign and religion.
  • Schedule: the availability blocks you enter, used to suggest date times.
  • Messages: texts, photos, videos and voice notes you send in chat.
  • Reports and blocks you make.

Collected automatically:

  • Location: with your permission, your phone's location. It reaches our server once and is stored only rounded to about 1 km, for matching by distance. Your phone's maps service (Apple or Google) turns it into a city name, and if you type a city instead, the search goes to OpenStreetMap.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system, push notification token, crash reports, and the IP address your requests come from (kept in server logs).
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches and the days you used the app.

From others:

  • Apple or Google, if you sign in with them: your name and email.
  • The public NPI Registry (NPPES), to check NPI numbers.
  • Apple or Google, when you buy something: purchase and subscription status (never your card details).

Earlier versions of the app asked for sexual orientation. We no longer collect it, and it was never shown to other members or used for matching.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: create your profile, show it to potential matches, match you, and let you message.
  • To verify you: check professional credentials and confirm you are the person on your ID.
  • To keep people safe: automatically screen new profile photos and prompt answers for explicit, violent or hateful content, review reports, hide or suspend accounts that break the rules, and prevent fraud and duplicate accounts.
  • To suggest date times and nearby date spots.
  • To send notifications about matches and messages.
  • To process purchases and keep the app working (fixing crashes).

We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to train AI models.

4. Face matching and biometric information

To confirm you are the person on your ID, you can choose an automatic face match or a review by a person.

If you choose the face match and tick the consent box, we send your ID photo and selfie to Amazon Rekognition (Amazon Web Services), which measures the geometry of the faces to compare them. Some state laws, including Illinois, Texas and Washington law, treat this as biometric information. Synapse never stores the face measurements; we keep only the result (a match score and the decision). Amazon processes the images for us under its service terms. In the same step, Amazon Textract reads the first and last name printed on your ID so we can check that it matches your license record. We keep only that name, and delete it together with your ID photo.

Retention and destruction schedule: we delete your ID photo and selfie within 30 days of the verification decision, and in any case within 3 years of when you submitted them. Deleting your account deletes them immediately.

We never sell biometric information, share it with other members, or use it for anything else. You can withdraw face-match consent at any time in Settings; later checks then go to a person. If you choose review by a person, no face matching is used.

5. AI features

  • Conversation starters: when you build your profile, we ask whether AI may use it to suggest openers, and you can change your answer any time with "Let AI suggest openers using my profile" in Settings. It stays off unless you allow it. If you allow it, your first name, job, prompt answers and profile photos are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to suggest openers to people you match with. Your private messages are never sent.
  • License reading: when you upload a license photo, we ask before sending it to Anthropic's Claude AI to read the license number, state and expiry date. You can type them yourself instead.

These providers process the data for us under their API terms and do not use it to train their models.

6. Who we share it with

Other members see your profile: first name, age, occupation, city, photos, prompt answers, verification badge, approximate distance and the optional details you add (such as religion or political views, which are always optional). Some matching settings, such as your gender, what you're looking for and who you want to see, are also available to other members' apps so matching works both ways. Other members never see your last name, exact location, ID, selfie, license details or schedule.

Service providers that process data for us:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, server functions), hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States.
  • Amazon Web Services: Rekognition for face matching and Textract for reading the name on your ID (both only with your consent), and, with Comprehend, for screening new profile photos and prompt answers.
  • Anthropic (conversation starters from your profile text and photos, only if you allow it, and license reading, only if you allow it).
  • Google Places (date spot suggestions, using the midpoint between two approximate locations).
  • Expo (push notifications and app updates).
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google (purchases and subscriptions).
  • Sentry (crash reports).
  • Resend (emails: sign-in codes, and messages to our team with verification documents for review and safety reports).
  • OpenStreetMap (city search) and your phone's maps service (turning location into a city name).
  • Apple and Google sign-in, if you use them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (we would tell you first).

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Messages: until either person in the conversation deletes their account. Unmatching or blocking closes the chat for both people; the messages are kept as evidence in case of a report.
  • ID photo and selfie: see the schedule in section 4.
  • Verification results, license details and reports: for the life of the account, to keep members safe.
  • Activity days: for the life of the account.
  • Crash reports: about 30 days. Server and email delivery logs: up to about 90 days.

When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your profile, photos, messages, matches, verification documents and consent records. Purchase records may remain with Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies, and an anonymous record of a subscription cancellation reason may remain. Copies in database backups are deleted when the backup is deleted.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: most details can be edited in the app.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account.
  • Copy of your data: email us and we will send it within 30 days (45 days where state law allows).
  • Withdraw consent: face matching and AI conversation starters can be turned off in Settings; location and notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or the EU/UK), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete and port your data, and to limit the use of sensitive information. We do not sell or share data for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of there.

To use a right, email support@synapsematches.com. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if we still decline, you can contact your state attorney general or data protection authority.

If EU or UK law applies to you: we process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), with your consent where we ask for it (face matching, AI conversation starters, location), for our legitimate interest in keeping members safe and preventing fraud, and where the law requires it. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. ID documents sit in a private storage area only our verification system can read, and database rules limit what each member can see. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

Synapse is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we delete it if we learn we have. Tell us at support@synapsematches.com if you think a minor is using Synapse.

11. Where data is processed

Synapse is based in the United States, and your information is stored and processed there. If you use Synapse from outside the US, your information is transferred to the US.

12. Changes

We will post any change here and update the date above. For important changes we will also tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
