The dating app for pharmacists, started by one

Synapse is a dating app for verified healthcare workers. Pharmacists verify with their NPI, everyone confirms their identity, and when you match, Synapse suggests times you're both actually off.

Verified with your NPI

Your NPI is checked against the NPPES registry maintained by CMS. It has to be active, and the name on it has to match your government ID. You can verify with a license photo instead, which a person on our team reviews. Then you match your ID to a selfie. Matches see your role and a verified badge, never your NPI, license or ID.

Built for pharmacy hours

Retail shifts that run past close, rotating weekends, overnights in the hospital, and holidays when the pharmacy stays open. Tick the mornings, afternoons and evenings you're usually free. When you match, Synapse suggests the times you're both free, and neither of you sees the other's full schedule.

Pharmacists, and the people you work alongside

Synapse is for every verified healthcare worker: pharmacists, physicians, residents, nurses, NPs, PAs, PTs, OTs, other licensed clinicians and healthcare students. You can filter by occupation and sub-specialty, from hospital and clinical to retail and community.

Started by a pharmacist and a physician

Synapse was founded by Jay Patel, PharmD, and Nishtha Gupta, MD. They watched friends and coworkers give up on apps that didn't account for their hours, or that let anyone claim a credential they didn't hold.

Free to use, with an optional upgrade

The free plan covers everything you need to meet someone: 3 likes a day, every filter, unlimited messaging with your matches, AI icebreakers, and suggested date times. Synapse+ adds unlimited likes, a view of who already likes you, and more, for $24.99 a month. The first 500 verified members get 3 months of Synapse+ free.

Questions pharmacists ask

Can pharmacy students join?

Yes. They join as healthcare students and verify with a .edu email or student ID.

Will my matches see my NPI or where I work?

No. They see your role and a verified badge. Profiles don't have a workplace field, so where you work stays private unless you share it.

I work nights or seven-on, seven-off. Does it still work?

Yes. Mark whichever mornings, afternoons and evenings you're free, and update them when your schedule changes.
