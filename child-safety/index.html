Safety

Child safety standards

Synapse - Dating for Healthcare Workers is a dating app for adults 18 and over, run by Synapse Connect LLC. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexual content involving a minor.

Only adults can use Synapse

Everyone must be 18 or older. We check each member's date of birth at sign-up, and verify their identity and healthcare credential before they can like or message anyone. We suspend the account of anyone we learn is under 18.

How to report

  • In the app: open the person's profile or your chat with them, tap Report and choose a reason. Use "Under 18" for anyone who may be a minor, and for any content involving a minor.
  • By email: write to help@synapsematches.com with "Child safety" in the subject. Please don't attach or forward the material itself; tell us who it involves and where you saw it.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. You can also report directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

What we do

  • We review child-safety reports before any others.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes in a reported chat are kept until the report is closed, or while the reported account is suspended, so they can be reviewed.
  • We suspend the account involved, which ends its chats, so the chat and its media can no longer be opened in the app.
  • We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to NCMEC, as US law requires.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement.

Contact

Our child-safety contact can be reached at help@synapsematches.com. They can answer questions about these standards and how we enforce them. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
