The dating app for doctors and residents

Synapse is a dating app for verified healthcare workers. Physicians verify with their NPI, everyone confirms their identity, and when you match, Synapse suggests times that fit around call, nights and weekends.

Verified in minutes with your NPI

Your NPI is checked against the NPPES registry maintained by CMS. It has to be active, and the name on it has to match your government ID. Add a selfie and, if you agree to the automatic comparison, most NPI holders are approved in minutes. Matches see your role and a verified badge, never your NPI or your ID.

Built for call schedules and residency

Q4 call, night float, and a schedule that changes every block. Tick the mornings, afternoons and evenings you're usually free, and update them when the rotation changes. When you match, Synapse suggests the times you're both free, and neither of you sees the other's full schedule. Then it suggests venues near a midpoint, so a first date doesn't mean crossing town after a 28-hour shift.

Not an empty doctors-only app

Apps for doctors only tend to be small. Synapse is for every verified healthcare worker: physicians, residents, nurses, NPs, PAs, pharmacists, PTs, OTs, other licensed clinicians and healthcare students. That's more people who understand your hours. If you only want to see physicians, filter by occupation and sub-specialty, which is free.

Started by a physician and a pharmacist

Synapse was founded by Nishtha Gupta, MD, and Jay Patel, PharmD. They watched friends and coworkers give up on apps that didn't account for call schedules, or that let anyone claim a credential they didn't hold.

Free to use, with an optional upgrade

Questions physicians ask

Can residents and medical students join?

Yes. Residents verify with their NPI. Medical students use a .edu email or student ID.

Is Synapse only for doctors?

No. It's for all verified healthcare workers, and you can filter by occupation if you want to.

Will my matches see my NPI or where I work?

No. They see your role and a verified badge, never your NPI, license or ID. Profiles don't have a workplace field, so where you work stays private unless you share it.

I'm matching into residency somewhere new. Does that matter?

Synapse is launching everywhere, and it shows verified healthcare workers near you, wherever you are.
