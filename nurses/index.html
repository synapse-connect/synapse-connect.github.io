The dating app for nurses, built around your shifts

Synapse is a dating app for verified healthcare workers. Nurses verify with their license, everyone confirms their identity, and when you match, Synapse suggests times you're both actually off.

Why nurses give up on dating apps

  • The schedule. Three 12s, nights, weekends, and holidays at the hospital. Most apps assume you're free at 7pm on a Tuesday.
  • Anyone can claim it. "I work in healthcare" in a bio proves nothing.
  • Dates that never happen. You match, you chat, and then you never find a night you're both off.

Verified the way nurses verify

Most nurses verify with a photo of their state license, and a person on our team reviews it, usually within a day or two. Expired licenses don't clear. Then you confirm your identity by matching a government ID to a selfie: AWS Rekognition compares them if you consent, or a person does if you'd rather. Your matches see your role and a verified badge, never your license number or ID.

How verification works →

Dates that fit three 12s and nights

In the Schedule tab, tick the mornings, afternoons and evenings you're usually free, day by day. When you match, Synapse compares both schedules and suggests the times you're both free. Your match only ever sees the overlap, never your whole schedule. Work nights? Mark the mornings and afternoons you're free instead.

Nurses, and the people you work alongside

Synapse isn't only for nurses. It's for verified physicians, residents, NPs, PAs, pharmacists, PTs, OTs, other licensed clinicians and healthcare students. Everyone you see understands the hours, and you'll see more people than a nurses-only app would show you. You can still filter by occupation if you want to.

Questions nurses ask

Can nurses join?

Yes. RNs, LPNs and LVNs, and NPs can all join, along with nursing students, who verify with a .edu email or student ID. You must be 18 or older.

How do nurses verify?

With a state license photo, which a person on our team reviews, or with an NPI if you have one. Everyone also matches a government ID to a selfie.

Can my matches see my license, my schedule or where I work?

No. They see your role and a verified badge. On schedules, they only see the times you're both free. Profiles don't have a workplace field, so where you work stays private unless you share it.

I'm a travel nurse. Can I use Synapse on assignment?

Synapse is launching everywhere, not region by region, and it shows verified healthcare workers near you, wherever your contract is.
