How Synapse verifies every healthcare worker

On most dating apps, "verified" means your selfie looks like your photos. On Synapse, it also means a credential was checked: your NPI against the national registry, or your license by a person on our team. Here's exactly what happens.

The steps

  1. 01

    Pick the credential that fits your role.

    That's an NPI for most physicians, residents, NPs, PAs and pharmacists, a state license photo for most nurses and other licensed roles, or a .edu email or student ID for healthcare students.

  2. 02

    We check the credential.

    NPIs are looked up in the NPPES registry maintained by CMS, and must be active and match the name on your ID. Licenses are reviewed by a person, and expired ones don't clear.

  3. 03

    You confirm it's you.

    You take a government photo ID and a selfie. With your consent, AWS Rekognition's facial similarity model compares them: strong matches pass, and borderline ones go to a person. Or choose a person from the start.

  4. 04

    You get a decision.

    With the automatic comparison, NPI holders can be approved in minutes. A .edu email is confirmed automatically, and a person reviews student IDs, license photos and anyone who chooses a person for the face check, usually within a day or two. If it doesn't clear, you're told why (the credential, the identity match, or both), so you know exactly what to send again.

  5. 05

    Your documents are deleted.

    Your ID photo and verification selfie are deleted within 30 days of the decision.

What each role verifies with

Role Credential Checked by
Physicians, residents, NPs, PAs and pharmacists An NPI. A license photo works too. An NPI is checked against the registry; a license photo is reviewed by a person
Nurses (RN, LPN/LVN) A state license photo, or an NPI if you have one A license photo is reviewed by a person; an NPI is checked against the registry
PTs, OTs and other licensed clinicians An NPI or a license photo An NPI is checked against the registry; a license photo is reviewed by a person
Healthcare students A .edu email or student ID A .edu email is confirmed automatically; a student ID is reviewed by a person

What your matches see, and what they don't

Matches see your role and a verified badge. They never see your NPI, license, ID or verification selfie. Those are stored separately from your profile photos, encrypted and with access restricted, and they're deleted within 30 days of the decision, or right away if you delete your account.

What verification doesn't do

We check professional credentials and confirm identity. We don't run background checks, and these checks don't reveal criminal history. Use your own judgment with everyone you meet. Blocking, reporting and other safety tools are always free.

Questions about verification

Why does Synapse need my ID?

To confirm the person holding the credential is you. A license or NPI alone doesn't prove that.

Can I skip the automatic face comparison?

Yes. Choose a person instead. It takes longer, usually a day or two.

What if my name changed?

The name on your NPI record or license has to match your ID. If they differ, contact help@synapsematches.com before you verify.

Can I delete my documents?

Delete your account in the app and we remove your profile, messages, photos and verification documents within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep them.
