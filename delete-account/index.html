Your account

Delete your Synapse account

This page explains how to delete your account in Synapse - Dating for Healthcare Workers, the app run by Synapse Connect LLC, and what happens to your data.

Cancel Synapse+ first

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Synapse+ subscription, because Apple or Google bills it. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, or you may still be charged.

In the app

Open Settings and tap Delete Account. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email help@synapsematches.com from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We reply to that address to confirm it's you, then delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it's done. We only act on a request we can confirm from the account's own address, so nobody can delete someone else's account. If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, say so in your email and we'll help you confirm the request.

What we delete

Your profile, photos, messages, matches, verification documents and consent records. Encrypted backup copies of our systems are kept for up to 14 days, so your data may remain in those backups for up to 14 days after deletion.

What we keep

  • Reports: kept after either account is deleted, without the names, as a safety record.
  • If the account was suspended, or a report about it is still open, a scrambled code made from its professional credential, which cannot be turned back into the credential. We use it only to spot the same credential on a new account.
  • A record of a suspension (the account ID), while the suspension lasts.
  • Purchase records with Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies, and an anonymous record of a subscription cancellation reason.

Section 7 of our Privacy Policy has the full details.
