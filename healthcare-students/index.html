The dating app for healthcare students

Synapse is a dating app for verified healthcare workers and the students training to join them. Verify with your school email, and when you match, Synapse suggests times that fit around class, clinicals and exams.

Get early access ↓

Free to join · The first 500 verified members get 3 months of Synapse+ free

For every healthcare program

Medical (MD and DO), nursing, pharmacy, PA, PT and OT, dental and public health students can all join, along with other healthcare programs. You must be 18 or older.

Verify with your school email

Enter your .edu email and type in the code we send you. If your school email doesn't end in .edu, upload your student ID and your university's name instead, and a person on our team reviews it, usually within a day or two. Everyone also matches a government ID to a selfie. Matches see "Healthcare Student" and a verified badge, never your school email or ID.

How verification works →

Dates that fit clinicals and exam weeks

Rotations change, clinical days start early, and exam weeks disappear. Tick the mornings, afternoons and evenings you're usually free, and update them when your schedule changes. When you match, Synapse suggests the times you're both free.

Meet people who get it

You'll see verified students and working clinicians: nurses, physicians, residents, pharmacists, PAs and more. Everyone understands why you can't do Thursday night. You can filter by occupation, including by program.

Free to use, with an optional upgrade

The free plan covers everything you need to meet someone: 3 likes a day, every filter, unlimited messaging with your matches, AI icebreakers, and suggested date times. Synapse+ adds unlimited likes, a view of who already likes you, and more, for $24.99 a month. The first 500 verified members get 3 months of Synapse+ free.

Questions students ask

Which programs count?

Medical, nursing, pharmacy, PA, PT/OT, dental and public health, plus other healthcare programs.

My school email doesn't end in .edu. Can I still join?

Yes. Upload your student ID and your university's name, and a person on our team reviews it.

Can I match with people who aren't students?

Yes. Synapse shows every verified healthcare worker, and you can filter by occupation.

When does Synapse launch?

Soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when you can download it.
