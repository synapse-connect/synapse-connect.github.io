    How it works

    Four steps from sign-up to a first date that actually happens.

    1. 01

      Verify your credentials and your face.

      Confirm your role with your NPI, a license photo, or, if you're a student, a .edu email or student ID. Then add a government ID and a quick selfie — compared by AWS Rekognition's facial similarity model if you consent, or by a person if you'd rather. With the automatic match, NPI holders and students with a .edu email can be approved in minutes; a person reviews everyone else, usually within a day or two.

    2. 02

      Build a profile that respects the work.

      Photos, prompts, and what kind of relationship you're looking for. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email — others see your role and a verified badge, never your license details or ID.

    3. 03

      Mark your schedule.

      In the Schedule tab, tick the mornings, afternoons, and evenings you're usually free. Once you match, Synapse uses both schedules to suggest times that actually work — not a 7pm Tuesday you'll never make.

    4. 04

      Match, then meet.

      When there's a mutual signal, you get venue ideas near a comfortable midpoint and times pulled straight from where your schedules overlap, plus AI-generated icebreakers if your match has turned them on. From there, it's your call.

    Verification

    Healthcare-grade trust, by default.

    The verification flow is the whole point. Here's exactly what we check and how.

    • NPI registry lookup Clinician NPIs are checked against the NPPES public registry maintained by CMS. The NPI must be active, and its name must match the name on your government ID.
    • License review If you verify with a license photo instead of an NPI, as most nurses do, a person on our team reviews it. Expired licenses don't clear.
    • Identity match A government photo ID is compared to a selfie. With your consent, AWS Rekognition's facial similarity model does the comparison: strong matches pass, and borderline cases go to a person. Or choose a person from the start.
    • Encrypted at rest, access-controlled Government ID photos and verification selfies are stored encrypted and access-controlled, and deleted within 30 days of the verification decision, or right away if you delete your account.
    • Profile photos stay separate from ID The name on your NPI record or license must match your name. Your verification selfie and ID are stored separately and never appear on your profile.
    • Specific feedback on rejection If verification doesn't clear, you'll get a structured reason (clinical credential, identity match, or both) so you know exactly what to re-submit.
    • Roles we verify Physicians, residents, nurses, NPs, PAs, pharmacists, PTs, OTs, other licensed clinicians, and healthcare students — each by the appropriate credential (NPI, license photo, or, for students, a .edu email or student ID).
    • Delete on request You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. We remove your profile, messages, photos, and verification documents within 30 days, save where law requires retention.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      Who can join Synapse?

      Synapse is for verified healthcare workers aged 18 and over: physicians, residents, nurses, NPs, PAs, pharmacists, PTs, OTs, other licensed clinicians, and healthcare students. Each person verifies with the credential that fits their role.

      How does Synapse verify healthcare workers?

      Clinicians with an NPI are checked against the NPPES registry maintained by CMS. If you verify with a state license photo instead, as most nurses do, a person on our team reviews it. Students use a .edu email or student ID. Everyone also matches a government photo ID to a selfie.

      Does Synapse run background checks?

      No. We check professional credentials and confirm identity, but those checks don't reveal criminal history. Use your own judgment with everyone you meet. Blocking, reporting, and other safety tools are always free.

      Is Synapse free?

      Yes. Free covers everything you need to meet someone: 3 likes a day, every filter, unlimited messaging with matches, and suggested date times. Synapse+ is optional, at $24.99 a month, $59.99 for 3 months, or $119.99 a year, billed through the App Store. The first 500 verified members get 3 months of Synapse+ free.

      Can my matches see my work schedule?

      No. You mark the times you're usually free, and Synapse compares both schedules when you match. Your match only sees the windows you're both free, never your full schedule.

      What happens to my ID and selfie?

      Government ID photos and verification selfies are stored encrypted and access-controlled, never appear on your profile, and are deleted within 30 days of the verification decision, or right away if you delete your account.
